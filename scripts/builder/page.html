The school

The name goes on the window, the installer and the shortcut.

Look

One colour. Everything else — buttons, menus, charts, printed pages — comes from it.

What this school gets

Anything switched off is not in the menu and is refused by the program, not merely hidden. Students, staff, attendance, classes and settings are always included.

Their data, ready before you send it

Attach the school's spreadsheets and the program arrives with everything already entered. Leave them empty and the client gets the setup wizard instead. CSV files; column names are matched loosely, in English or Arabic.

Keep it on their computers

A locked copy opens only on computers you have activated. Copying it — the installer, the installed program or its data — to another computer gives that computer a different code, and it will not open there.

Optional. One per line. Those computers open it straight away. Any other computer shows its code the first time the program starts — activate it with Activate a computer on the right.
